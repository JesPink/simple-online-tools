Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to use the tools?

No! All our tools are completely free and require no sign-up or account creation. Just visit the tool page and start using it immediately.

Is my data safe when using your tools?

Yes. Most of our tools process your data entirely within your browser - it never leaves your device. For tools that require server processing, we don't store your data and follow strict privacy practices. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use these tools for commercial purposes?

Absolutely! Our tools are free for both personal and commercial use. There are no licensing restrictions or hidden fees.

Why did you create Simple Online Tools?

We believe everyone should have access to high-quality, fast, and privacy-focused online tools without the hassle of sign-ups, ads, or complex interfaces. Our mission is to provide simple, effective tools that just work.

How can I request a new tool?

Use the contact form above with "Feature Request" as the subject, or email us directly. We prioritize tools that would be most helpful to our users.
