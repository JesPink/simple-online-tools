Privacy Policy

How we protect your privacy and data

Effective Date: September 24, 2025

Contact: simpleonlinetool.com@gmail.com

1. Overview

Simple Online Tools ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our website and online tools at simpleonlinetool.com.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Client-Side Processing

Most of our tools process data entirely in your browser - this means your text, files, or other inputs never leave your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Data We DO Collect

  • Website Analytics: We use Google Analytics (anonymous mode) to understand website usage patterns
  • Server Logs: Basic technical information like IP addresses, browser type, and access times
  • Contact Form Data: When you contact us, we store your email and message content

2.3 Data We DO NOT Collect

  • Personal information like names, addresses, or phone numbers
  • Content you input into our client-side tools
  • Files you process through our tools
  • Account information (we don't require accounts)

3. How We Use Data

The limited data we collect is used for:

  • Analytics: Understanding which tools are most popular to improve our service
  • Technical Support: Debugging issues and improving website performance
  • Communication: Responding to your inquiries and support requests
  • Security: Protecting against abuse and maintaining service availability

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: Website usage analytics in anonymous mode. Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare: Content delivery and security services. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Basic website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Google Analytics tracking (anonymous)

Your Consent: By using our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect website functionality.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Client-Side Data: Processed locally and never stored by us
  • Analytics Data: Retained by Google Analytics for 26 months
  • Server Logs: Retained for 90 days for security purposes
  • Contact Form Data: Retained until resolved, then deleted within 1 year

Deletion Requests: Contact us at simpleonlinetool.com@gmail.com to request deletion of any personal data we may have.

7. Security Practices

  • HTTPS encryption for all website traffic
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Limited data collection minimizes security risks
  • Client-side processing keeps your data on your device

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Users

Our website is hosted and operated in the United States. If you are accessing our service from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will:

  • Update the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy
  • Notify users of significant changes through website notices
  • Maintain previous versions for reference if requested

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the United States and the state of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.
